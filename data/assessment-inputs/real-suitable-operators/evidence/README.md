## Official catalogue description at snapshot
A suitable operator is a person or corporation who has been registered by the **Department of the Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation** as being suitable to carry out an environmentally relevant activity (ERA). The suitable operator register is required under the _Environmental Protection Act 1994_.
