## Official catalogue description at snapshot
Register of licensed builders, trade contractors, designers, nominees, site supervisors and occupational licence holders maintained by the Queensland Building and Construction Commission (QBCC).

All resources are refreshed daily.

Register of licensed builders and contractors = all non-cancelled QBCC Act licences, including both active and suspended. Current-state snapshot.
Suspended Licences = licences suspended at any point during the current financial year to date. Event list, includes licences since reinstated.
Cancelled Licences = licences cancelled at any point during the current financial year to date. Event list.

For up-to-date information on an individual licensee, refer to the QBCC's licence search.

## Official selected resource description at snapshot
QBCC Act licences that are not cancelled, including both active and suspended licences, as at the date of publication. Details include licence number, name of licensee, business address, licence class and financial category.

Refreshed daily. For up-to-date information on an individual licensee, refer to the QBCC's licence search.
